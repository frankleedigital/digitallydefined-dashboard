The 5-Layer Defense — Do This Before a Scam Happens

Build Your Fortress

L1 Dedicated "bait" account — a separate email/phone that never holds your real SSN, bank login, or 2FA. Use it only for public signups, mailers, and anything you won't miss.
L2 One password = one site. No reuse. A password manager turns a 40-site nightmare into a 30-second login ritual.
L3 Bank "instant fraud number" — program a direct line on your phone that skips the 20-minute hold and reaches a fraud agent who can freeze and chargeback on the spot.
L4 Family code word — pick a phrase only your immediate circle knows. No "grandma, it's me" gets past it. If no code word, no transfer. Period.
L5 The 24-hour rule — no money moves within 24 hours of any "urgent" call, email, or text. Sleep on it. Most real opportunities can survive one night.
