The 5 Estate Documents
Gen X Women Actually Need

A will is just the beginning. Here's the complete set that protects your money, your wishes, and your family — in 60 days, at any income level.

1
Last Will & Testament
Names your executor, directs where assets go, appoints a guardian for any minor children. Does not avoid probate.
Cost: ~$100–$400 (online) or via attorney
2
Durable Power of Attorney
Lets a trusted person manage your finances if you're incapacitated — while you're still alive. Without it, family may need a court-appointed guardian to access your own accounts.
This is the document almost everyone skips. Don't.
3
Advance Healthcare Directive
Names who makes medical decisions for you and states your treatment wishes (comfort care vs. life-prolonging measures). No one else should have to guess in the ICU.
Decide now. Write it down. File it.
4
Revocable Living Trust
Holds your major assets, bypasses probate, keeps things private, and gives you control over timing. Worth it if you own a home.
Attorney package: ~$1,500–$4,000 one-time
5
Beneficiary Designations
Your 401(k), IRA, and life insurance beneficiary forms override your will. An ex-spouse named in 2009 can still inherit your IRA. This is a 15-minute audit — and the single highest-return task on this list.
Free. Do it today, before you book any attorney.
